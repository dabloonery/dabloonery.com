Tirekick

Checks prices and flags likely scams while you shop secondhand.

Chrome extension Beta

  • Puts a score on each eBay, Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist listing you look at.
  • Compares the asking price with what the same item sold for on eBay.
  • Flags listings that look like scams, with the reasons.
  • Reseller mode helps you work out what something will sell for after repairs.
  • Optional AI write-ups, with your own API key or a model on your computer. Off until you turn it on.

How I build

Every tool has to follow these.

  1. It does its job first. Features that get in the way don't ship.
  2. No pop-ups or nagging.
  3. Nothing happens with your data, AI or money without asking you first.
  4. No account unless the tool really needs one.
  5. A subscription only when there's an ongoing cost, like a server.
  6. Open source, Apache 2.0.
  7. A small bit of fun somewhere.
